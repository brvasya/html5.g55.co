DYING LIGHT: THE BEAST: BEAT EM UP
An unofficial fan-made HTML5 arcade reimagining.

PLAY
Upload all extracted files to one folder on a static web host.
Open index.html through that host. All assets and fonts are local;
no external runtime libraries or game services are required.
For a local preview, run a static HTTP server in the extracted folder.
Direct file:// loading has not been verified.

KEYBOARD
WASD / Arrow keys  Move and change lanes
J                 Machete combo (tap or hold)
K                 Knockback kick
Space             Dodge in the movement direction
E / L             Beast mode when the amber meter is full
Escape / P        Pause and resume
M                 Mute / unmute
F                 Fullscreen, where supported

TOUCH
Use the movement stick and HIT, KICK, DODGE, and BEAST buttons.
Touch controls appear on coarse-pointer devices and small viewports.

CAMPAIGN
Five chapters, three connected areas per chapter, 64 infected, including five bosses.
Clear each encounter to advance. Cleared routes remain open behind you.
Break each setting's supply containers for health and Beast charge.
Kills restore a little health. Beast mode increases damage and reduces
incoming damage for 7.5 seconds. Dodging briefly avoids damage.

Progress saves at chapter entry and completion in browser-local storage.
Retry restores chapter-entry health, charge and score. Next chapter restores
40 health. Resize and fullscreen preserve the current run.
