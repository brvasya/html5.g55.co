GEARS OF WAR: BEAT EM UP

A fan-made, illustrated HTML5 arcade brawler.

PLAY
Extract this ZIP and upload its contents to an ordinary web-hosting folder.
Open index.html through that web server. All runtime files use relative paths;
no installation, build step, or external runtime service is required.

CAMPAIGN
5 chapters, 3 areas per chapter, 88 enemies including 5 bosses.
Jacinto Ruins > Timgad Station > The Hollow > Imulsion Refinery > Locust Stronghold.
Clear each area, then move right. Chapters resupply health and ammunition.
A checkpoint is saved at the start of each chapter in this browser.
Retry restores that chapter-entry checkpoint, including score and supplies.

CONTROLS
WASD / Arrow keys: move and change lanes
J: three-hit melee combo
K: chainsaw attack (45 charge; melee hits recharge it)
L: Lancer fire (hold)
Space: dodge roll
R: reload; press again inside the white zone for a damage bonus
Escape / P: pause
M: mute sound
F: fullscreen, where supported
Touch: movement stick, strike, saw, fire and dodge buttons; tap R to reload.

RELEASE
index.html is at the ZIP root. Assets and the Russo One font are bundled.
The release includes a full-color 170 x 128 thumb.png.
Runtime art uses separate indexed PNG sheets with native transparency.
Each walking actor has exactly two distinct opposite-step walk drawings.
Scenery uses separate far, middle and near parallax layers plus ground.
Each setting has a distinct prop with intact, damaged and broken states.
More Games uses the G55.CO tracking convention with this game's full title.
