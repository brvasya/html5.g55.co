CALL OF DUTY MODERN WARFARE 4: BEAT EM UP
HTML5 arcade fan game — version 1.0.2

SOUND UPDATE
Added distinct gunfire, strike, kick, reload, dodge, damage, Overdrive,
footstep, supply-break, and menu effects. Audio activates from Start or
keyboard/touch input. The Sound button confirms unmuting with a short tone.

ARTWORK FIX
Corrected indexed-palette transparency in characters, props, and scenery.
All 29 game images preserve their intended solid and transparent pixels.

PLAY
Upload the contents of this folder to any static web host and open index.html.
Keep the assets folder beside index.html. All runtime files are local and use
relative paths; no build step, account, external CDN, or Flash plugin is needed.

For local testing, open a terminal in this folder and run:
  python3 -m http.server 8000
Then visit http://localhost:8000 in a modern browser.

KEYBOARD
WASD or arrow keys  Move in two directions across the combat lane
J                  Strike; chain three hits for a finisher
K                  Heavy kick
L                  Fire rifle
R                  Reload
Space              Dodge
E                  Overdrive when the meter reaches 100 percent
Esc or P           Pause / resume
M                  Toggle sound

TOUCH
Use the left joystick to move and the labeled buttons to fight, dodge,
reload, and trigger Overdrive. Portrait and landscape layouts are supported.
The top-right controls provide pause, sound, and fullscreen where supported.

CAMPAIGN
Five chapters: Blackwater Docks, Urban Siege, Dust & Thunder, Cold Signal,
and Final Transmission. Clear three sectors and defeat the commander in
each chapter. Reach the right-hand extraction point to continue.

Watch amber attack telegraphs. Break chapter-specific supplies for health
and ammunition. Cleared sectors stay open for backtracking. Each new chapter
restores 40 health, reloads the rifle, and grants 36 reserve rounds. Retry
restores the current chapter checkpoint. Best score and sound preference are
saved in this browser when local storage is available; run progress lasts
for the current page session.

FILES
