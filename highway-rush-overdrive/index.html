HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

A/D or ←/→: steer. S/↓: brake. Touch: drag the road or use arrows.

OVERDRIVE

Space/Shift or tap meter to boost. Near misses charge boost and keep combos alive. Boosted passes earn more.

PROGRESSION

600 m per level; 10 levels, then endless. Challenges chain for +500 each. Finish 3 / 8 / 15 to unlock faster cars you can select during a run.
