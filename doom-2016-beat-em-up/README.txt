DOOM 2016: BEAT EM UP
G55.CO | UNOFFICIAL FAN GAME

An illustrated Adobe Flash-style HTML5 arcade game.
Five chapters, fifteen areas, 85 demons, five boss encounters, and a Cyberdemon finale.

PLAY
Extract the complete ZIP, then open index.html in a modern browser.
For hosting, upload all files together to any folder and open its index.html.
No build tools, CDN, server API, or external runtime dependencies are required.
Keep the assets and fonts folders beside index.html.

KEYBOARD
WASD or arrow keys: Move / change lane
J: Melee combo (hold for two punches and a glory kick)
K: Guard; tap just before a front attack to parry
L: Shotgun
E: Berserk when the rage meter reaches 100%
Space: Dash
Esc or P: Pause / resume
M: Mute
F: Fullscreen

TOUCH
Use the left joystick and labeled action buttons.
Touch controls appear on touch devices and compact layouts.

CAMPAIGN
Clear every demon in an area, then move right to continue.
Melee kills restore health. Every kill restores one shotgun shell.
Hits and kills charge Berserk, which empowers melee for seven seconds.
Destroy themed supplies for health and ammunition.
Guard front-facing attacks and projectiles. Dash out of red ground slams.
Each new chapter grants up to 40 health and 10 shells, plus full guard.
Continue and Retry restore the chapter-entry checkpoint.
The browser stores progress and mute preference locally when available.

CONTENTS
index.html, game.css, engine.js, game.js, asset-catalog.js
assets/: 30 indexed PNGs; native transparent cutouts; at most 16 colors each
game.css: embedded local Oxanium font; fonts/OFL.txt: SIL Open Font License
thumb.png: full-color 170 x 128 thumbnail

VALIDATION
Campaign simulation completed all fifteen areas through ordinary game controls.
Chromium browser checks covered desktop, portrait, compact landscape, touch input,
fullscreen, menu layouts, pause, loading, chapter transitions, retry and outcomes.
All five prop types and their three damage states were visually inspected.
Both HTTP subfolder play and direct local-file play were verified.
These checks do not cover every physical phone or browser implementation.
