HALF-LIFE 2: BEAT EM UP
G55.CO | UNOFFICIAL FAN GAME

Play the five-chapter campaign through City 17, Route Kanal, Ravenholm,
Nova Prospekt and the Citadel. Clear all enemies in each area and move
right to continue. Each chapter has a boss and its own breakable supplies.

KEYBOARD
WASD or Arrow keys: move and change lanes
J: crowbar combo (hold or tap; third hit is a finisher)
K: heavy strike
L: gravity blast (30 energy; pushes enemies and deflects projectiles)
Space: dodge with brief invulnerability
Esc or P: pause/resume
M: mute/unmute
F: fullscreen where supported

TOUCH
Use the left movement stick and the four right-hand action buttons.
Controls appear on touch devices and compact viewports.

PROGRESSION
Melee kills restore a little health. Gravity energy recharges.
Smash supplies and walk over pickups to collect them when needed.
A new chapter restores 45 health and fills gravity energy.
Continue and Retry use the chapter-entry checkpoint, saved in this browser.

HOSTING
Extract this archive into one directory on a static web host. Open its
index.html over HTTP or HTTPS. All runtime artwork, scripts and fonts are
included and use relative paths, including when hosted in a subfolder.
For local testing, run: python3 -m http.server 8000
Then open http://localhost:8000 in your browser.
Direct file:// play has not been verified.

FONT
Nimbus Sans Narrow, packaged locally. See fonts/LICENSE.txt.
