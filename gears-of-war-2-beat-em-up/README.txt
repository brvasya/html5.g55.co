GEARS OF WAR 2: BEAT EM UP

An unofficial fan-made arcade reimagining.

PLAY
Upload the contents of this folder to a static web host and open index.html.
All artwork, code, and fonts are included. No installation or external runtime
downloads are required. Subfolder hosting is supported.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrow keys: move along the arena and between lanes
J: three-hit melee combo
K: chainsaw attack (35 fury)
L: fire the Lancer
R: reload the 12-round magazine
Space: dodge
Esc / P: pause
Touch screens: left joystick and right action buttons

Match an enemy's lane before attacking. Red warnings announce attacks.
Break chapter supplies for health and ammunition. Eliminate every Locust
in an arena, then advance right. Each chapter has three connected arenas
and a boss encounter. Health and supplies recover at chapter transitions.

The current chapter checkpoint is saved locally when browser storage is
available. Retry restores chapter-entry score and supplies. Start Game
begins a new campaign; Continue restores your current checkpoint.

Use the top-right controls for sound and fullscreen where supported.
The included thumb.png is a full-color 170 x 128 thumbnail.
