RESIDENT EVIL 4: BEAT EM UP

PLAY / HOST
Extract the complete ZIP before playing. Open index.html in a modern browser,
or upload the extracted contents to any static web host and open index.html.
Keep the assets and fonts folders alongside index.html, game.js and game.css.
No installation, build step, server application, account or external CDN is needed.
For iframe embedding, allow fullscreen on the iframe.

CAMPAIGN
5 chapters, 15 areas, 94 enemies and 5 chapter bosses.
El Pueblo / Lake and Quarry / Salazar Castle / Island Stronghold / Final Escape.
Each chapter has its own breakable supply container and scenery.
Clear an area, then move right. Continue after each chapter boss.
Defeat allows a retry from the beginning of the current chapter.

CONTROLS
WASD / arrow keys: move
J: knife combo (third strike is a finisher)
K: roundhouse kick
L: handgun
Space: dodge
R: reload
Escape / P: pause or resume
Touch: movement stick and action buttons; tap R to reload.
Sound and fullscreen controls are available on the game screen.
